# Lizenzhinweise / License notices

## Deutsch

- **3MF Katalog Manager:** MIT; siehe [App-Lizenz](https://github.com/Bexxs75/3mf-katalog-manager/blob/master/LICENSE) und
  [Quellcode mit Lizenz](https://github.com/Bexxs75/3mf-katalog-manager/blob/master/LICENSE).
  Weitere App-Abhängigkeiten stehen in [THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md](https://github.com/Bexxs75/3mf-katalog-manager/blob/master/THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md).
  Das OCI-Label `licenses=MIT` bezeichnet die App, nicht sämtliche Image-Komponenten.
- **LinuxServer.io baseimage-kasmvnc:** eigenes Projekt mit eigenen Lizenzdateien;
  [Quellcode und Lizenz](https://github.com/linuxserver/docker-baseimage-kasmvnc).
- **KasmVNC:** separate Komponente;
  [Quellcode und Lizenz](https://github.com/kasmtech/KasmVNC).
- **Ubuntu-Pakete:** unterschiedliche Lizenzen je Paket. Die installierten
  Lizenztexte liegen unter `/usr/share/doc/<paket>/copyright` im Image.
  [Ubuntu-Paketverzeichnis](https://packages.ubuntu.com/noble/).
- **s6-overlay:** separate Komponente;
  [Quellcode und Lizenz](https://github.com/just-containers/s6-overlay).

## English

- **3MF Katalog Manager:** MIT; see the [app license](https://github.com/Bexxs75/3mf-katalog-manager/blob/master/LICENSE) and
  [source license](https://github.com/Bexxs75/3mf-katalog-manager/blob/master/LICENSE).
  Additional app dependencies are listed in [THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md](https://github.com/Bexxs75/3mf-katalog-manager/blob/master/THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md).
  The OCI `licenses=MIT` label identifies the app license, not every image component.
- **LinuxServer.io baseimage-kasmvnc:** a separate project with its own license files;
  [source and license](https://github.com/linuxserver/docker-baseimage-kasmvnc).
- **KasmVNC:** a separate component;
  [source and license](https://github.com/kasmtech/KasmVNC).
- **Ubuntu packages:** licenses differ by package. Installed license texts are in
  `/usr/share/doc/<package>/copyright` inside the image.
  [Ubuntu package directory](https://packages.ubuntu.com/noble/).
- **s6-overlay:** a separate component;
  [source and license](https://github.com/just-containers/s6-overlay).

Download the test data: [3MF-Testdaten.zip]({{testdaten}}).

Open the folder "Downloads" in the Finder and double-click `3MF-Testdaten.zip`. Drag the new folder `3MF-Testdaten` into the folder **Documents**.

**Important:** do **not** extract the ZIP and 7z files inside the folder `3MF-Testdaten` – the app needs them as they are: `Testkatalog-Backup.zip`, everything in `2 Archive` and in `3 Fehlerdateien`.

Leave the folder where it is for the whole test. The folder `1 Katalog` becomes your test catalog during the test.

If your antivirus reports files from `3 Fehlerdateien` (e.g. `Pfad-Ausbruch.zip` or `Verschluesselt.7z`), that's fine: they are broken on purpose. Please don't delete them, or they will be missing in tests K1 to K4.

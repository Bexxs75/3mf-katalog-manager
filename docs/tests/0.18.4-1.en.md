# 1 · New in 0.18.4: Preview images and spool groups

## N18440 · Old preview images are rendered again once
Steps:
1. Install the test version over an existing 0.18.3 with a catalog that holds STL and 3MF models with rendered preview images. Note beforehand what an angular STL tile looks like.
2. Start the app and wait until the background previews are done.
3. Start the app again.
Expected: Rendered preview images of STL and 3MF models are rendered again once; flat faces of angular parts then have clearly different brightness. Custom images and preview images embedded in 3MF files stay unchanged. On the second start nothing is rendered again.
Note: STEP files and very large meshes (over 200 MB) keep their previous image.

## N18441 · Apply print temperatures to all spools of a group
Steps:
1. In Material Manager, add a filament spool with quantity 3 (same material, manufacturer, color and location).
2. Edit one of the three spools, enter nozzle and bed temperatures and leave the box "Apply print settings to all 3 spools in this group" checked. Save.
3. Open the other two spools. Then edit one spool again, uncheck the box, change the temperatures and save.
4. Edit a spool, change its color and watch the box.
Expected: After step 2 all three spools have the same four temperature values; remaining weight, location and image of the other spools stay unchanged. Without the check only the edited spool changes. As soon as color, material, manufacturer, location or diameter are changed in the form, the box disappears. Single spools without a group show no box.
